Spiced Chia Pudding

Vegan. Make overnight. Grab and go!

Buy Ingredients at Whole Foods

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup chia seeds
  • 15 oz light coconut milk (1 can)
  • 1/2 cup oat milk
  • 3 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamom (optional)

Directions

1. In a large bowl, mix all ingredients together, stirring continuously for 5 minutes.
2. Let sit for 5 minutes.
3. Stir again to prevent clumping.
4. Transfer each portion to an individual jar.
5. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight.
6. Add fresh fruit, granola, nuts, or any other toppings. Enjoy!
